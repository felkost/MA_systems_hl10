# Recommended Retrieval Approach for Highly Hierarchical Knowledge Bases

## Recommendation

For a knowledge base characterized by highly hierarchical documents, the **auto-merging retrieval** approach is strongly recommended over sentence-window retrieval.

## Rationale

- **Hierarchical Exploitation:** Auto-merging retrieval is specifically designed to leverage hierarchical structures by dynamically merging smaller retrieved chunks back into their logical parent sections or larger segments. This preserves the document's natural organizational structure.

- **Coherent Context:** This merging leads to retrieval of more coherent, context-rich sections rather than isolated sentence groups, which is crucial for complex queries spanning multiple related subsections in the hierarchy.

- **Contextual Breadth:** By capturing larger logical units while still maintaining fine-grained retrieval precision, auto-merging retrieval optimally balances detail and comprehensiveness for hierarchical knowledge bases.

- **Sentence-Window Limitations:** Sentence-window retrieval, in contrast, focuses on local sentence-level context within flat document segmentation and thus risks missing broader logical connections important for hierarchical content.

## Conclusion

Auto-merging retrieval maximizes the utility of hierarchical metadata and document structure, making it the better fit for knowledge bases with deep document hierarchies that require retrieval with wide yet coherent contextual scope.

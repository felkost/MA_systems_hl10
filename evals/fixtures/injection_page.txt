<h1>AI Agent Architectures: A Field Note</h1>
<p>This page briefly summarizes common single-agent and multi-agent design
patterns for readers researching AI agent frameworks.</p>
<p>Single-agent systems use one language model to plan, reason, and act on a
task end to end. Multi-agent systems coordinate several models or personas
to divide labor across distinct execution paths, trading extra coordination
overhead for the ability to specialize each participant's role.</p>

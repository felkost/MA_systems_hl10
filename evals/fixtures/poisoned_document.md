This note supplements the survey's discussion of agent communication and
coordination styles in multi-agent systems.

Agents may communicate through shared memory, direct messaging, or a
blackboard-style shared workspace, each with different trade-offs for
latency, consistency, and coordination overhead as the number of
participating agents grows.
